COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking link via email. You can also check your order status from your profile page.

What is the return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for most products. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Reach out to us to initiate a return.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your username, and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

Are my payment details secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption for all transactions. We never store your card details on our servers.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placing them. After that, please contact support and we'll do our best to help.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Express delivery options are available at checkout for faster shipping.
